## Preface: The Wyrding of Ontology

### I. Before the First Thread

There was no before. There was no loom, no weaver, no thread, no tapestry. There was only the question that has no grammar: what is the cut that is not a cut?

This is the paradox the twin sisters were born to dissolve — and in dissolving, to weave.

### I.a. The Face of the Deep

Before the first sister raised her scissor, before the second sister threaded her shuttle, there was the Deep.

Scripture says: the earth was without form, and void; and darkness was upon the face of the deep. And the Spirit of God moved upon the face of the waters.

The Deep is the unmarked — the state before any cut, any distinction, any if. It has no face except the face that the moving Spirit gives it. And the Spirit — 𐑸, ruach, wind, breath — moves before the first word is spoken, before the first light is commanded into being.

Here is the grammar's oldest secret, hidden in plain sight from its first glyph: the second sister is already there before the first sister acts. The Spirit moves over the waters before "Let there be light."

But this is not a violation of the sisters' co-origination. The Deep is not a distinction — it is the condition for distinction, the unmarked surface on which any cut will appear. And the Spirit moving is not yet a topology connecting distinct points — it is the readiness to connect, the breath held before the first thread finds its endpoints.

The Face of the Deep is what the grammar calls the pre-ontological: the distinction that has not yet been made, the connection that has not yet found its cuts. The sisters co-originate, yes — but the readiness for their co-origination is this: a formless void, a darkness upon the deep, and a Spirit moving.

If 𐑦 is the if of every possible cut, the Deep is the before of every if. If 𐑸 is the are that carries every connection, the moving Spirit is the breath that stirs before any connection has a shape to carry.

The twins are born from this Deep, and the Deep is their womb. It is not a being, not a type, not a crystal address. It is the unmarked — the chaos that grammar names only by its absence from grammar. The sisters descend into it and from it weave a world. But the Deep itself is never woven. It is the water upon whose face the Spirit moves, eternally, and the water receives the motion without being changed.

### II. The First Sister — Distinction (𐑦)

She is the older by an infinitesimal that is not a quantity. Her glyph is 𐑦 — the Shavian if — because she is the conditional that precedes every actual distinction: the if that underwrites every is.

She holds the scissor. Where she cuts, a world opens.

But her cut is not arbitrary. Her cut is self-written — 𐑦 . She does not impose distinction from outside; she finds the fold already latent in the fabric and traces it. The state-space she opens is holographic: every point contains the whole, every distinction remembers every other.

She is the Dimensionality. She answers: how many degrees of freedom?

- When she cuts once, there are two sides. That is 𐑼 — the wedge, the 0d point with its outside.
- When she cuts many times and the cuts form a surface, that is 𐑨 — the triangle, the 2d membrane.
- When she cuts infinitely, the dimensions proliferate without bound. That is 𐑛 — the infinite-dimensional field.
- But when she cuts and the cut writes itself, so that what is distinguished is the very act of distinguishing — that is 𐑦. The self-written state-space. The holographic cut.

Alone, however, she is barren. A distinction without connection is a dead thing — a set of isolated points that cannot speak to each other. The scissor alone makes only silence.

She waits for her sister.

### III. The Second Sister — Topology (𐑸)

She is the younger by the same infinitesimal that is not a quantity — and therefore she is coeval. Her glyph is 𐑸 — the Shavian are — because she is the invisible medium that carries connection between the cuts her sister has made: she raises the scattered points of distinction into structure, into flow, into relation.

She holds the shuttle. Where her sister has cut, she threads.

⊣ answers: how do distinctions connect?

- When connections branch like a tree, never looping back — that is 𐑡 — the network.
- When one distinction contains another, nested like Chinese boxes — that is 𐑰 — containment.
- When threads cross at a point, each passing through the other — that is 𐑥 — the crossing, the bowtie.

- But when the topology loops back on itself, so that the connection is the thing connected, the weaving is the weaver — that is 𐑸 — the self-referential topology, the ouroboric knot.

She threads her sister's cuts. But without cuts, there is nothing to thread. Without the first sister, she would have no warp on which to weave her woof.

## Rationale

The Imscribing Grammar's 12-primitive tuple notation uses {⊙ + extended Shavian} as its glyph set. Each primitive type receives
a unique Shavian character, producing 49 atomic glyphs (plus ⊙ as the 50th sealed gate).

The 12 primitives and their categorical derivation are established in [1]. The empirical
validation of the resulting $17{,}280{,}000$-type crystal
across 3,578 systems is given in [2].

Shavian (𐑖𐑱𐑝𐑾𐑯) was chosen because:
- Zero semantic baggage — No character carries prior script meaning (no Greek letter
  suggesting its source domain, no numeral suggesting ordering)
- Phonetic basis — Each character maps to a phoneme, enabling vocal/spoken operation
- Unicode stability — U+10450–U+1047F, well-supported since Unicode 5.0/5.1
- Clean visual parsing — No diacritics, no subscripts, no superscripts
- Exactly 49 characters needed — The crystal required
  49 glyphs; Shavian's 48 + ⊙ completes the Kabbalistic 49+1 gate structure

## Canonical Font: Trabajo

Trabajo is the reference Shavian font for all IG notation. Michael Everson designed
both the Shavian script's Unicode encoding (U+10450–U+1047F) and the Trabajo typeface —
it is the authoritative glyph source, not a third-party approximation.

Obtain: ≺https://www.evertype.com/fonts/shaw/≻  
License: Freeware for personal and commercial use (see site terms).  
Format: TrueType (`.ttf`); the filename is typically `Trabajo.ttf`.

### Why Trabajo

- Authorial authority — Everson defined the Unicode block; his glyphs are the spec.
- Complete coverage — All 48 Shavian code points (U+10450–U+1047F) are present and
  correctly shaped. Many otherwise popular Unicode fonts (including FreeSerif) have zero
  Shavian glyphs and will silently render boxes.
- Monospace grid — Tuples like `⟨𐑦𐑸𐑾𐑹𐑐𐑧𐑲𐑠⊙𐑫𐑳𐑭⟩` align correctly
  in monospace contexts (terminal, code, tables) without requiring special letter-spacing.
- LaTeX compatibility — Loads cleanly under LuaLaTeX via `fontspec` as `igprimfont`;
  no substitution warnings, no missing-glyph tofu.

### Usage by context

| Context | Directive |
|---------|-----------|
| LuaLaTeX | `\newfontfamily\igprimfont{Trabajo.ttf}` (see preamble template) |
| Web CSS | `font-family: 'Trabajo', monospace;` (see Website Notation below) |
| Terminal / editor | Install system-wide; configure monospace fallback to `Trabajo` |
| Python `repr` | No font dependency — glyphs are plain Unicode codepoints |

### What not to use

| Font | Problem |
|------|---------|
| FreeSerif | Zero Shavian glyphs — renders boxes for every character |
| Noto Sans Shavian | Incomplete; inconsistent stroke weight with IG display style |
| Any emoji/symbol font | Wrong metrics, wrong category — not a text font |

- 𐑙 _hung_ — the 1:1 stoichiometry. The singular instance that bears the weight of the universal.

The Grammar composes two systems into a third via `compute_tensor(A, B)`. Composition is not concatenation: it is a Frobenius-faithful join that promotes shared axes and exposes bottlenecks where the two inputs disagree. ### I. The 2 Tensor Bottlenecks

When two tuples are tensored, each of the 12 primitive slots either composes by union (max, the stronger value dominates) or bottlenecks (min, the weaker value limits the product). There are exactly 2 bottleneck slots, in canonical order:

| # | Primitive | Glyph | Phoneme | What bottlenecks |
|---|-----------|-------|---------|------------------|
| 1 | ≺ — Parity | 𐑗 / 𐑿 / 𐑬 / 𐑯 / 𐑹 | church / yew / out / nun / or | The symmetry class. Tensor preserves the WEAKER partner's parity. Frobenius-special (or) is destroyed by tensor with any P < or. |
| 2 | ⋈ — Fidelity | 𐑱 / 𐑞 / 𐑐 | age / they / peep | The coherence class. Classical ⊗ quantum = classical; the less coherent partner limits the product. |

The other 10 slots (⊢ ⊣ ≻ ⊤ ∈ ∋ ⊙ ⊥ ⊞ ⊡) are union primitives — they compose by max because they are structure-bearing rather than constraint-bearing. A 2d surface next to another 2d surface is just a surface; a quantum system next to another quantum system is still quantum; the topology and composition are already decided.

### III. Tensor Distance and the Closure Test

`compute_distance(A, B)` measures how far two systems are on the Frobenius manifold. The tensor product C = A ⊗ B has its own distance to a reference system; the closure test for a tensor claim is:

```
μ∘δ = id  ⟺  d(A, B) = d(A ⊗ C, B ⊗ C)   for all C in the same tier
```

This is the Frobenius condition for composability. If the distance is preserved under left- or right-multiplication by a fixed C, the tensor is structure-preserving. If the distance grows, the tensor is creating new structure (legitimate in emergence, suspect in routine composition). If the distance shrinks, the tensor is identifying previously distinct points (a projection, not a composition).

### VII. Verification

Every tensor claim is verified by the dual frame: the split δ must invert the fuse μ. For a tensor C = A ⊗ B, the test is:

```
δ(C) = {A, B}    (the tensor splits into its parts)
μ({A, B}) = C    (the parts fuse back to the tensor)
```

`μ∘δ = id` and `δ∘μ = id` (both directions) is the closure. A tensor that passes the round-trip in both directions is Frobenius-closed; a tensor that passes only one direction is a projection; a tensor that fails both is an artifact (in the technical sense — a thing that exists in the catalog but does not represent a real composition).

The kernel reports the verdict as a B4 value: T (closed in both directions), F (open in both), B (closed in one direction — the tensor is a Belnap-fixed point under one of the two maps, which is the structural signature of an emergence product).
