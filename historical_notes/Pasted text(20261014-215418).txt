## 1. The Horn-Torus Pinch as the Critical Fixed Point

### 1.1 Geometry of the horn torus

The horn torus with major radius equal to minor radius is parametrized by

$$\text{horn}(R, u, v) \;=\; \bigl(R(1 + \cos u)\cos v,\; R(1 + \cos u)\sin v,\; R\sin u\bigr),$$

where $u$ is the poloidal angle and $v$ is the toroidal angle. The two radii are equal — the horn torus is the limiting case of the standard torus in which the hole has shrunk to nothing and the surface touches itself along a single circle.

At the poloidal angle $u = \pi$, the entire toroidal circle collapses:

$$\text{horn}(R, \pi, v) \;=\; (0, 0, 0) \quad \text{for every } v.$$

This is the **pinch point** — the self-contact of the horn torus. The poloidal circle at $u = \pi$ has radius $R(1 + \cos \pi) = 0$, so all toroidal angles $v$ map to the same point.

The criticality primitive is assigned the value ⊙. This glyph denotes a self-referential fixed point — a critical configuration that reproduces itself under the flow. The pinch is where the horn torus touches itself, so the fixed point is not a single point on a circle but a one-parameter family of identified points. ### 2.2 Ascent and descent

There is an **ascent** map

$$\mathrm{embed}_{k,n} : \mathrm{FDE}(k) \to \mathrm{FDE}(n) \qquad (k \le n),$$

which is injective but not surjective for $k < n$. Each deeper scale adds at least one incomparable middle value.

There is a **descent** map

$$\mathrm{restrict}_{n,k} : \mathrm{FDE}(n) \to \mathrm{FDE}(k) \qquad (k \le n),$$

which sends a middle value with index less than $k$ to the corresponding middle value in $\mathrm{FDE}(k)$, and sends a middle value with index at least $k$ to the top element. The descent is a genuine coarsening: for $k < n$, it is not injective.

### 2.3 The round-trip identity

The composition

$$\mathrm{restrict}_{n,k} \;\circ\; \mathrm{embed}_{k,n} \;=\; \mathrm{id}_{\mathrm{FDE}(k)}$$

The descent is also transitive, so an arbitrary-depth walk is well defined: the route taken through intermediate scales does not matter, only the endpoints.

$$t \;=\; \frac{\pi}{3} + \frac{2\pi k}{3}, \qquad k \in \mathbb{Z}.$$

$$t \;=\; \frac{\pi}{3}, \quad t \;=\; \pi, \quad t \;=\; \frac{5\pi}{3}.$$

$$n \;\longmapsto\; (n \bmod 16, \; n \bmod 3).$$

$$\mathbb{Z}/48 \;\cong\; \mathbb{Z}/16 \times \mathbb{Z}/3,$$
